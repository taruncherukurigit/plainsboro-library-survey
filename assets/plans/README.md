# Floor plans

## Included here

| File | Source | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| `ground-floor.png` | Extracted from the NetSpot project export | Zone 1 survey basemap |
| `second-floor.png` | Extracted from the NetSpot project export | Zone 2 survey basemap |

These are the exact images the survey was calibrated and plotted against, pulled from
`Maps/` inside the `.netspu` archive. Scale was calibrated from the graphic scale bar
printed on each drawing.

## Not included

**Third floor (children's level).** Obtained during preparation but not surveyed —
that floor was excluded by choice. If you add it later for the predictive model, save
it here as `third-floor.png`.

**Site plan and cross section.** Referenced in the analysis (the site plan places
attached single-family residences adjacent to the building, which explains the
residential SSIDs in F-06; the cross section confirms the double-height central volume
behind F-05) but not reproduced here.

## Attribution

All drawings originate from *Architectural Record*, "Plainsboro Public Library",
16 March 2011. Building by BKSK Architects, completed May 2010 for the Township of
Plainsboro.

Reproduced for non-commercial technical analysis with attribution. They are design
drawings, not as-builts — note the "Future Aquarium" label on the children's level
plan. Fifteen years of a working library means furniture has moved and rooms have been
repurposed.
