## Material deliberately excluded

**Architectural photography** by Jeffrey Totaro and Vanni Archive is *not*
reproduced in this repository or on the project site. These are commissioned
professional photographs. They were consulted to confirm construction materials and
the double-height central volume, and that use is cited in the analysis — but the
images themselves are not republished.

**Internal network addressing.** Screenshots showing the library's client IP,
gateway, DNS and subnet mask are excluded from all published material. This is the
library's infrastructure and is not mine to circulate.

**Identifiable people.** No photographs containing recognisable library patrons
appear in any published material.
